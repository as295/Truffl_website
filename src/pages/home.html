Frequently
asked questions

  • Truffl is designed to integrate into your existing stack rather than force you to replace it.

    You can connect your existing telephony, CRMs, support systems, APIs, internal tools, databases, and business workflows. Truffl agents can retrieve context, call tools, update systems, transfer conversations, and trigger actions across your existing infrastructure.

    For enterprises with more complex requirements, Truffl can also support custom integrations and deployment workflows.

  • You do not need to rebuild everything from scratch.

    Existing prompts, workflows, knowledge bases, APIs, call logic, and provider configurations can be migrated progressively. Teams can start with a single use case or traffic segment, evaluate performance against their current system, and expand once the new deployment meets their production requirements.

    The objective is to make switching platforms measurable and incremental rather than a high-risk migration.

  • Truffl is designed to give developers control over both agent behaviour and the underlying runtime.

    Teams can configure the agent’s instructions, models, speech providers, tools, knowledge, conversation behaviour, transfer logic, runtime settings, and deployment configuration.

    Truffl provides an opinionated platform where useful, without turning the agent into a black box. Developers can inspect, test, evaluate, and iterate on how the system behaves in production.

  • Deployment is only one part of the system.

    Truffl brings the build, deployment, observation, evaluation, and improvement loop into the same platform. Teams can review conversations, evaluate agent behaviour against defined criteria, identify failure patterns, test changes, and use those learnings to improve subsequent versions of the agent.

    Instead of prompts, analytics, QA, and production behaviour living in separate tools, Truffl is being built around a continuous learning loop.

  • Yes. Truffl is being built for production voice workloads where reliability, latency, observability, control, and operational visibility matter.

    The platform is intended to support both inbound and outbound agents, human handoffs, integrations, configurable infrastructure, monitoring, evaluation, and enterprise-specific workflows.

    Teams can begin with a focused deployment and expand usage as performance, reliability, and operational requirements are validated.
