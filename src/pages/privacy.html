2. The roles of Truffl and its customers

Truffl processes personal data in different roles depending on the context.

2.1 When Truffl determines the purpose

Truffl generally acts as the data fiduciary or controller for personal data relating to:

  • website visitors;
  • access applicants and sales enquiries;
  • customer account administrators and authorised workspace users;
  • billing, contracting and business contacts;
  • security, service integrity and legal compliance; and
  • Truffl's own communications and marketing.

2.2 When an enterprise customer determines the purpose

When a Truffl customer uses the Services to operate a voice agent, customer-support workflow, revenue-recovery workflow, embedded application experience or other interaction, that customer generally determines why the end user's personal data is processed. In that context:

  • the customer acts as the data fiduciary or controller; and
  • Truffl acts as the customer's data processor or service provider and processes the data on documented instructions, subject to the applicable agreement.

The customer's privacy notice and choices govern its collection and use of end-user data. Questions or requests concerning a customer-operated interaction should normally be directed to that customer. We will assist the customer as required by law and our agreement.

If Truffl and a customer determine purposes jointly in a particular deployment, the applicable contract should allocate the parties' responsibilities.

3. Personal data we collect

Depending on how you interact with Truffl, we may collect the following categories.

3.1 Information you provide directly

  • name, work email address, telephone number, company, role and location;
  • access-request, contact-form and meeting-scheduling information;
  • pricing-calculator inputs, selected models, generated quotes, timestamps and a browser identifier used to maintain quote records;
  • account credentials and authentication information;
  • communications, support requests, feedback and survey responses;
  • contracting, billing and transaction information;
  • configuration information, prompts, workflows, evaluation criteria and instructions submitted to the Services; and
  • any other information you choose to provide.

3.2 Voice and interaction data

Where enabled by a customer deployment, the Services may process:

  • live audio and voice recordings;
  • transcripts and conversation summaries;
  • speaker turns, timestamps, interruptions and other interaction events;

  • tool calls, actions, approvals, fallbacks, transfers and outcomes;
  • quality reviews, evaluation scores, annotations and reviewer comments; and
  • technical metadata such as call status, duration, routing, latency and provider events.

Whether a call is recorded, and what notice or consent is required, depends on the customer's configuration and applicable law. Customers are responsible for providing legally required notices and obtaining legally required permissions before using Truffl to place, receive, record or analyse communications.

3.3 Workspace and integration data

  • workspace membership, roles and permissions;
  • agent versions, deployment settings and operational history;
  • data exchanged with integrations selected by the customer;
  • API requests, SDK events, webhook payloads and associated metadata;

  • usage information relating to features and service performance.

3.4 Automatically collected website and device data

  • IP address, device and browser type, operating system and approximate location;
  • pages viewed, referring URLs, timestamps, clicks and navigation events;
  • cookie identifiers, advertising identifiers and campaign attribution data;
  • login, authentication and security events; and
  • diagnostic, crash, performance and fraud-prevention data.

Our use of cookies and similar technologies is described in the Cookie Notice.

3.5 Data obtained from third parties

We may receive personal data from:

  • the organisation that provides you access to Truffl;
  • customers that configure Truffl interactions;
  • identity, authentication and security providers;
  • telephony, speech, language-model, hosting, analytics, advertising and scheduling providers;
  • connected applications and data sources selected by a customer;

  13. create aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify an individual.

5. Legal grounds

We process personal data only where permitted under applicable law. Depending on the jurisdiction and context, the basis may include:

  • your free, specific, informed and unambiguous consent;
  • processing necessary to provide a service you requested or to take steps at your request;
  • certain legitimate uses recognised by applicable law;
  • our legitimate interests, where applicable and not overridden by your rights, including operating and securing the Services, responding to business enquiries and improving product performance;
  • performance of a contract;
  • compliance with a legal obligation; or
  • establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it using the method presented at collection, through cookie settings where relevant, or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully completed and may limit features that depend on the relevant data.

6. AI and automated processing

Truffl uses automated systems and third-party models to interpret interactions, generate responses, select workflows, evaluate quality and support actions. These systems can make mistakes.

Customers configure the permitted scope of their agents, connected tools, approval requirements, fallbacks and human handoffs. Customers must not use Truffl as the sole basis for decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on individuals unless the use is lawful, appropriately governed and expressly supported by the applicable agreement.

Customer Content and model training: Truffl will not use Customer Content to train a general-purpose model for unrelated customers unless the customer has expressly agreed in writing. Aggregated or de-identified service information may be used to improve reliability, safety and performance where it cannot reasonably identify a person or customer.

7. How we disclose personal data

We may disclose personal data to:

7.1 Customer-authorised recipients

The customer that controls a workspace and its authorised users may access data processed within that workspace according to assigned permissions.

7.2 Service providers and subprocessors

We use vendors to provide infrastructure, hosting, storage, telephony, speech processing, language models, communications, authentication, monitoring, analytics, advertising, scheduling, customer support and professional services. They may process personal data only for authorised purposes and subject to contractual safeguards appropriate to their role.

A current list of material subprocessors, their processing purposes and relevant processing locations is available to customers on request. Where required by an applicable agreement, we will provide notice of material changes to that list.

7.3 Business and professional advisers

We may disclose information to auditors, insurers, banks, lawyers, accountants and other professional advisers where reasonably necessary.

7.4 Legal, safety and compliance recipients

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, respond to lawful requests, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or security incidents, or enforce agreements.

7.5 Corporate transactions

Information may be disclosed in connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganisation, sale of assets or similar transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

7.6 Advertising and analytics partners

If you consent where required, website identifiers and activity may be disclosed to analytics and advertising partners for measurement, attribution, personalisation or cross-context behavioural advertising. Depending on applicable law, this may be considered a "sale," "sharing" or targeted advertising even where no money changes hands. You may reject non-essential cookies or change your preferences at any time. Where required, we will honour recognised opt-out preference signals.

We do not knowingly sell voice recordings, transcripts or Customer Content for monetary consideration.

8. International processing and transfers

Truffl and its providers may process personal data in India and other countries where they operate. Those countries may have different data-protection laws.

Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, we will use appropriate contractual or legal mechanisms. For EEA, UK or Swiss data, these may include adequacy decisions, approved standard contractual clauses and supplementary measures. Transfers from India will remain subject to restrictions or requirements notified under applicable Indian law and any sector-specific localisation obligations that apply to the customer.

Customers requiring a specific processing region or data-residency arrangement must ensure it is stated in the applicable Order Form or Data Processing Addendum.

10. Security

We use administrative, organisational and technical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, acquisition, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction. Measures may include access controls, authentication, encryption in transit and where appropriate at rest, tenant separation, logging, monitoring, vulnerability management, backups, incident response and vendor review.

No service is completely secure. Customers are responsible for protecting credentials, configuring permissions, reviewing authorised integrations and notifying us promptly of suspected compromise.

We will notify affected customers or individuals and relevant authorities of qualifying personal-data breaches as required by applicable law and contract.

12. Children

The website and Services are intended for businesses and authorised adult users, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children for account creation or marketing.

Customers must not configure Truffl to process children's personal data unless the deployment has been expressly approved in writing, the customer has established a lawful basis, required verifiable parental or guardian consent has been obtained, and all additional safeguards required by law are implemented.

If you believe a child has provided personal data directly to Truffl without appropriate authorisation, contact us.
