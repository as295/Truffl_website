Cart recovery

Policy

Workflows › Cart recovery › Policy

Saved 2m ago

When a cart is abandoned and the customer has given consent, call inside contact hours and establish what stopped the purchase.

— Confirm the cart with get_cart before saying anything about it.
— Ask why the purchase stopped. Do not lead with a discount.
— Check serviceability before promising a delivery window.
— Offer only what list_offers returns for this customer.
— Confirm before any action is taken, and record the outcome against the run.
— Hand off to a person on refusal, dispute or anything outside policy.
