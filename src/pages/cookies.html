1. About this notice

This Cookie Notice explains how Oravio AI Private Limited, operating under the brand Truffl ("Truffl", "we", "us" or "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and related online services.

It should be read with the Truffl Privacy Policy.

2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a browser or device. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, local storage, software-development-kit identifiers and scripts that recognise a browser or device, remember preferences, measure activity or support advertising.

Some technologies are operated directly by Truffl. Others are operated by third parties whose services appear on or support the website.

Cookies may be:

  • Session cookies, which expire when the browser closes; or
  • Persistent cookies, which remain until their stated expiry or until deleted.

They may also be:

  • First-party cookies, set by Truffl; or
  • Third-party cookies, set by another provider.

3. Categories we use

3.1 Strictly necessary

These technologies are required for the website or service to function securely. They may support:

  • login and session management;
  • authentication and account security;
  • load balancing and network delivery;
  • fraud, abuse and bot prevention;
  • saving privacy choices;
  • form submission and essential error handling; and
  • maintaining requested functionality.

They cannot be disabled through the consent interface because the requested service may not work without them. They should not be used for advertising.

3.2 Analytics and performance

These technologies help us understand how visitors use the website, which pages and campaigns perform, where errors occur and how the experience can be improved. They may collect page views, navigation events, referral data, approximate location, device information and pseudonymous identifiers.

Analytics technologies will remain disabled until consent where required.

3.3 Functional

These technologies remember optional choices or enable enhanced third-party features, such as embedded scheduling, video or personalised interface settings.

If a third-party embed can track visitors before interaction, it must remain blocked until the visitor consents to the applicable category or actively chooses to load it.

3.4 Advertising and retargeting

These technologies may:

  • attribute visits or enquiries to campaigns;
  • measure advertisement effectiveness;
  • build or use audiences;
  • limit advertisement frequency;
  • personalise advertisements; or
  • recognise a visitor across websites, applications or devices.

Information disclosed for cross-context behavioural advertising may be treated as "sale" or "sharing" under some privacy laws, even where no money is exchanged.

Advertising and retargeting technologies will remain disabled until consent where required. Visitors may reject them without losing access to the public website.

4. Consent choices

On the first applicable visit, Truffl will display a consent interface offering equally clear choices:

  • Accept all
  • Reject non-essential
  • Manage preferences

No analytics, functional tracking or advertising/retargeting technology may activate before the required choice is recorded.

The preference centre must allow a visitor to enable or disable each non-essential category independently. Strictly necessary technologies remain active.

Consent must not be obtained through pre-ticked boxes, inactivity, scrolling or a design that makes rejection materially harder than acceptance.

5. Changing your preferences

You can withdraw or change consent at any time through the Cookie Settings link displayed in the website footer.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already completed lawfully. After withdrawal, Truffl must stop loading the relevant non-essential technologies and, where technically possible, delete their first-party identifiers. You may also delete cookies through browser settings.

Blocking all cookies through a browser may affect login or other essential functionality.

6. Global Privacy Control and advertising opt-outs

Where required by applicable law, Truffl will treat a recognised browser-based opt-out preference signal, such as Global Privacy Control, as a request to opt out of sale or sharing and cross-context behavioural advertising for the associated browser or device.

If Truffl becomes subject to a law requiring a separate Your Privacy Choices or Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information link, that link must be presented in the footer and connected to the consent platform.

Truffl does not sell personal data for monetary consideration. Its use of advertising or retargeting providers may nevertheless constitute sharing, targeted advertising or a sale under certain laws.

7. Embedded content and scheduling

The website may contain embedded video, product demonstrations or scheduling functionality such as Calendly. These providers may set cookies or receive device and interaction data.

The recommended implementation is:

  1. keep the embed inactive before the required consent;
  2. show a privacy-preserving placeholder;
  3. load the embed only after consent or an informed user action; and
  4. provide a direct external link where appropriate.

Submitting Truffl's contact form and agreeing to its stated processing does not automatically constitute consent to unrelated advertising cookies.

8. Current cookies and providers

The Cookie Settings interface available in the website footer displays the current cookies and similar technologies used on the device, including their provider, category, purpose and duration. That interface forms part of this Cookie Notice and is updated when our website technologies change.

Truffl reviews its website and consent configuration when providers or functionality change. A technology will not be classified as strictly necessary merely because it is convenient for analytics or advertising.

9. Retention of choices

Truffl will retain a record of the visitor's consent or rejection for the period reasonably necessary to honour and demonstrate the choice. The recommended renewal period is 12 months, or earlier if the purposes or providers materially change.

Cookie durations must not exceed what is necessary for their stated purpose. The exact duration of each technology must appear in the cookie inventory.

10. International processing

Third-party analytics, advertising and embedded-service providers may process information in countries outside the visitor's location. Such processing is addressed in the Privacy Policy and applicable provider terms and safeguards.

11. Updates

We may update this Cookie Notice when technologies, providers, purposes or legal requirements change. The current version and date will be posted on the website. If a change materially expands non-essential processing, we will request consent again where required.
